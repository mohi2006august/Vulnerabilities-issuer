# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want BugRaiser to be a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience, background, identity, or location.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good faith; ask before assuming bad intent.
- Give and accept feedback graciously.
- Respect maintainers' time. Many are volunteers.
- Follow the [Responsible Use Policy](docs/RESPONSIBLE_USE.md) in everything you do with this tool.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or doxxing
- Using the project to attack, extort, or pressure others
- Sharing vulnerabilities, credentials, or personal data publicly without following the disclosure policy
- Spamming maintainers with automated or unverified reports
- Encouraging or assisting unauthorized access to systems

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, and issues; warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants. Reports go to `conduct@your-domain.example` and are handled confidentially.

Enforcement steps: (1) private warning → (2) temporary restriction → (3) permanent ban. Severe violations may skip steps.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces and when representing the project publicly.
